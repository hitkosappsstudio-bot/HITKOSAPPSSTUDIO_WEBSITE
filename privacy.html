Cookies Policy

HITKOS APPS STUDIO uses cookies to improve your browsing experience and deliver relevant content. Cookies are small data files created by a web server and stored on your device that help us track usage patterns and recognize return visitors.

We use the following tracking technologies:

Cookies

Help track online preferences, identify return visitors, and customize your web experience so you don't need to re-enter information on every visit.

Web Beacons

Programming code used to track individual visits and online behavioral patterns for service improvement and marketing purposes.

IP Address Tracking

Used to identify server issues, manage problems, and collect demographic data for better service delivery.

Log Files

Used to track and monitor member usage patterns across our website for analytics and security purposes.

Third-party advertising companies may also place cookies on your browser when ads are displayed. You can choose to disable cookies in your browser settings; however, some website features may not function correctly as a result.
